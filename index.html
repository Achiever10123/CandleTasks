    Using the app

    • Add a task: type it and press Enter. Plain words work: call Sam fri 3pm, pay rent monthly on the 1st, gym every mon wed fri, water plants 3 days after done, !high for priority. A preview shows what was understood.
    • Tags: write #work in the input to tag a task. Click any tag to filter by it.
    • Edit: click a task's text to rename it.
    • Reorder: drag a task up or down. Pinned tasks stay on top.
    • Pin: the pin icon keeps a task at the top of its list.
    • Details: the chevron opens notes, steps, tags, repeat, due date and move-to-list.
    • Due time: add a time to get an alert at that moment. Overdue tasks show Tomorrow and Next week buttons to snooze them.
    • Views: Today, Overdue and Next 7 days gather tasks from every list. Click a list tab to go back.
    • Priority & sort: set Low / Medium / High (the checkbox ring changes color), then sort by priority or due date next to the search box.
    • Tag colors: right-click any tag to pick its color.
    • Mini mode: the chevron beside Minimize shrinks the window to a quick input and today's tasks.
    • Move: drag a task onto another list's tab to move it.
    • Repeat: daily, weekly, or on days you pick (like Mon/Wed/Fri). A done task comes back by itself.
    • Lists: use the tabs for separate boards. Double-click a tab to rename it.
    • Archive: the box icon archives a task. Open "Archive" in the footer to restore tasks, delete them for good, or use Delete all.
    • Undo: after archiving or deleting, tap Undo in the message or press Ctrl+Z.

    Streak

    • Complete at least one task a day to grow your streak. You have a 2-day grace period.
    • Click the flame to see your week, month and stats.
    • Every 7 days you earn a streak freeze (up to 3). A freeze covers an extra missed day so your streak survives.
    • The flame changes color as the streak grows, with a confetti burst at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 and 365 days.

    Other

    • Sync: in Settings, choose a folder inside Dropbox or OneDrive to share your tasks and streak between computers.
    • Tray: with "Minimize to tray" on, right-click the tray icon for Quick add. Overdue tasks show as a number badge.
    • Weekly review: on Sundays you get a summary notification. Click it to open your stats.
    • Launch at login: Settings; works in the installed app.
    • Sound: the speaker icon in the header mutes or unmutes sounds.
    • Compact mode: smaller rows for a small pinned window (Settings).
    • Colors: pick a theme color or choose your own in Settings.
    • Opacity: make the window see-through in Settings.
    • Backup: Settings has one-click backup and restore of all your data.
    • Overdue: you get a desktop alert when a task becomes overdue, and a red count in the footer.
